# POPULOS services package — solution gating

| Product | Include when | Do not infer from |
|---|---|---|
| Sustentação da plataforma de endpoint | the client wants POPULOS to operate the endpoint platform after implementation | an implementation project without continuous operation |
| Sustentação do ambiente Veeam | the client wants POPULOS to support the Veeam environment | a Veeam license renewal alone |
| Sustentação de firewalls Fortinet | the client wants POPULOS to take over FortiGate support from another partner | the presence of FortiGate firewalls |

Every product here requires `governance.support_terms` as an approved `commitment` (hours, coverage, response and resolution times). The institutional SLA table covers failures of implementation services only; it is not a support SLA.
